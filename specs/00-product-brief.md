# TrackRanker product brief

## Product name

TrackRanker

## Target users

Competitive 100m, 200m, and 400m sprinters who train under the guidance of a coach.

## Problem statement

Sprint athletes can complete demanding prescribed sessions without a clear understanding of each session's purpose or a structured record of how the work felt and progressed. That gap can weaken confidence and make consistent improvement harder to see.

## Proposed solution

TrackRanker will give athletes a clear, supportive place to understand prescribed sessions, record training evidence, reflect on confidence, and recognise consistent process. It complements coaching rather than replacing or judging it.

## Product principles

- Support, never undermine, the coach-athlete relationship.
- Make training purpose and progress understandable.
- Reward healthy process and consistency rather than dangerous overtraining.
- Keep reflection quick, respectful, and useful.
- Never diagnose injury or encourage an athlete to train through pain.
- Protect athlete data and communicate uncertainty honestly.

## Initial feature boundaries

The intended first product version will focus on prescribed sprint sessions, completed repetition data, effort and difficulty, post-session notes, confidence reflection, consistency evidence, and process-based gamification.

## Explicitly excluded from the first version

- Medical diagnosis, rehabilitation plans, or return-to-play decisions
- Advice that overrides a coach or autonomously prescribes training
- Social feeds, public leaderboards, or athlete-to-athlete comparison
- Wearable-device integrations and automated timing hardware
- Nutrition plans, payment processing, and team administration
- Predictive injury or performance claims

Milestone 2 implements the first bounded product capability: CRUD for prescribed training sessions and their intended purpose. Repetition results, reflection, confidence, and gamification remain deferred.
